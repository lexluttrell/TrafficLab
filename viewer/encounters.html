Source, measurement definitions & reproducibility

US DOT / FHWA NGSIM, eastbound I‑80 in Emeryville, April 13, 2005. This is a five-minute development sample from a roughly 500 m observation site, not today’s traffic or a calibrated simulation. Three examples were selected by the predeclared coverage/time/ID rule. A fourth repeated-change example was added under a documented exploratory amendment, using the same ranking within that cohort. No selection uses the behavioral response.

Positions refer to vehicle front centers. Bumper gap subtracts the leader’s length from front-to-front spacing. Time gap divides that distance by follower speed. Missing leaders and invalid geometry produce missing values. Speeds are source estimates; acceleration and jerk effects have not been analyzed.
