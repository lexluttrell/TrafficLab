KEEP THE EXPERIMENT HONEST

Data, checks & uncertainty

Explore the evidence ↓

Does it still resemble the observations?

Every run is compared with the same 18 fully observed eastbound PeMS station-bins from September 24. Demand comes from measured entry counts, with inferred exits. Bindings and merge lengths are provisional.

These same-day discrepancies are diagnostics, not a pass certificate. Altered driving is a counterfactual: lower error does not prove a better driver model. There is no independent westbound score.

How stable is the result?

We repeat each setting with seeds 42, 43 and 44 while keeping departure times, routes and the selected car fixed. Below are paired differences in trip time, including delay before insertion.

The opposite-direction group has 173 cars that cannot directly meet the target. Its nonzero changes expose random-stream divergence. This post-run diagnostic is shown for every setting; nearby-group differences include that stochastic noise. Three seeds are a small sensitivity check, not a confidence interval. They do not cover structural model error.

What was checked?

    The gap behind belongs to the closest reported follower whose SUMO leader is your car; that identity can change. Gaps are net bumper distances along the route. Missing leaders and followers stay missing; the search distance is 200 m. Time gaps below 2 m/s are not calculated.

    All drivers use the existing Krauss car-following and LC2013 lane-changing models. Only the target’s tau and speedFactor change. The reference target has a fixed speed factor of 1.0. Its desired pace differs from the older replay’s randomly assigned preference. No emotional contagion, calming response, or fitted “swimming” behavior is claimed.
