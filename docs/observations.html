Detector records describe aggregate traffic. They do not show which individual cars were present, and reported speeds may include estimation. Station-to-road matching, timestamp conventions, data coverage and redistribution terms still need review against the actual files.
The simulation remains synthetic and uncalibrated. These observations will support later calibration and held-out validation; simply loading data does not perform either.
